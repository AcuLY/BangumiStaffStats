## Requirements
### Requirement: Person detail SHALL use one closed operation contract

The request SHALL contain required shared `query`, required
`input.personId`, and optional shared `PersonDetailViewV1`. It SHALL reject
`refreshCollection`, mode, image URLs, frontend revision, Drawer state, and
unknown fields. Scope/section-specific sort values SHALL fail with a stable
field error.

#### Scenario: Refresh is supplied
- **WHEN** a person-detail request contains `refreshCollection`
- **THEN** the closed request SHALL reject it before collection or Archive evaluation

### Requirement: Complete evidence SHALL remain separate from section view

The success data SHALL contain one minimal person reference, complete summary,
metrics, bounded tags, rating distribution/timeline, and personal-only global
comparison and preference evidence. These values SHALL not change with
section, search, sort, order, or page.

Works SHALL be a closed subject/series union. Contributions SHALL be a closed
staff/cast union preserving requested PositionKey, exact staff member or
character/role evidence, `provenance=exact`, and series-only contribution
workCount. Characters SHALL carry stable identity, primary role, workCount,
and exact raw-Subject appearances.

#### Scenario: A staff set contributes one work
- **WHEN** the requested staff-set identity matches an exact member credit
- **THEN** the contribution SHALL keep the staff-set PositionKey and the actual exactPositionKey
- **AND** it SHALL not fabricate a staff-set raw credit

### Requirement: Detail projection SHALL be deterministic and bounded

Works and characters SHALL use their accepted scope/section sort sets,
missing-last strict total order, normalized search, checked pagination, and
at most 20 returned items. Search and pagination SHALL not recompute complete
metrics or evidence. An out-of-range page SHALL be a 200 empty item list with
the searched total.

#### Scenario: A valid page is beyond the result
- **WHEN** page offset starts after the searched section
- **THEN** items SHALL be empty while complete evidence and pagination total remain valid

### Requirement: Person-detail generation SHALL be capability isolated

Deterministic personal/global/error goldens SHALL be consumed across Go and
TypeScript. Generators SHALL hash only `/person-detail` and its transitive
owned graph, leaving query, catalog, rankings, and candidates bytes unchanged.

#### Scenario: An unrelated endpoint changes
- **WHEN** another capability adds paths or schemas
- **THEN** person-detail generated bytes SHALL remain unchanged
