## Why

The clean-room production implementation has drifted from immutable oracle commit `644b7748674e553f863d0ffd61d029f86fdc0717` in visible hierarchy, geometry, copy, responsive behavior, and interaction, even though the formal rewrite was required to change internal architecture only. Two live backend success envelopes also violate their already-accepted contracts, preventing unmodified co-star and person-detail flows from reaching the surfaces that must be compared.

This change restores the original formal-development boundary now: the oracle remains the external-behavior authority; real APIs and explicitly approved production capabilities remain, but they may differ only inside their authorized slots and may not redesign or displace surrounding oracle behavior.

- Repair the 779/780/781 boundary, desktop query background isolation, compact ranking layout shift, unnamed controls, prohibited ARIA, contrast, effective hit targets, and production chunking defects revealed by the audit.
- Add deterministic component/browser acceptance that distinguishes `PRESERVE_ORACLE`, `INTENTIONAL_DELTA`, and `NEW_CAPABILITY`, and rejects every unclassified outward difference.

Externally visible classification:
